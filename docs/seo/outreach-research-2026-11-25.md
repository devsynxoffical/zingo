# zingoassist.us: link-building and citation research

Research date: 2026-11-25. Method: WebSearch (standard mode). Every URL below appeared in search results. Nothing was opened, submitted or contacted. Costs and terms come from search snippets, so check the live page before acting. No contact names or emails were invented. "Find contact page" means no contact route was visible in the results.

Asset shorthand:
- **RULES**: /real-estate-assistant-rules plus the 8 state pages (FL, TX, CA, GA, NC, AZ, NY, CO)
- **ROI**: /real-estate-va-roi-calculator
- **TC-PDF**: /tools/transaction-coordinator-checklist
- **TASKS-PDF**: /blog/real-estate-virtual-assistant-tasks-checklist
- **SERVICE**: the company and service pages themselves (for listings and inclusion requests)

## Suggested priority

1. **Citations first (free):** GBP (service-area), Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, BBB free profile, Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush, Crunchbase.
2. **RULES pages are the strongest linkable asset.** NAR's chart is from 2020. Pitch an "updated 2026 state rules" angle to Inman, Clever, Realtor.com and FastExpert, to brokerage knowledge bases like Radius, and to journalists via Qwoted.
3. **TC checklist:** ListedKit, DocJacket.
4. **ROI calculator:** AgentFire, myRealPage, Rex.
5. **Roundup inclusion:** The Close, RISMedia, Joingenius, Outsource Accelerator's US VA guide.
